# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is an accessibility project, and the people it serves are often on the receiving end of carelessness. Do not add to that here.

## Expected

- Assume good faith and extend it.
- Accept that someone's access needs are real whether or not you share them.
- Take disagreement on the merits, and let it end when it is settled.
- Describe the problem, not the person.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning language about disability, and ableist framing of accessibility work as charity or as a burden.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through [paracorp.io](https://paracorp.io). Reports stay confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this, and may ban anyone whose behaviour warrants it. Decisions get an explanation where one is possible, though a report's details stay private.
